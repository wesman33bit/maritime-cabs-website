What Maritime Cabs is

Maritime Cabs is a technology platform that connects passengers with independent local taxi companies operating in New Brunswick, Canada. Maritime Cabs does not itself operate taxis or employ drivers. Each ride is provided by an independently licensed taxi company using the Maritime Cabs platform to receive bookings.

Booking a ride

When you request a ride, you receive an estimated fare based on your pickup and destination. The ride is offered to drivers at a participating taxi company covering that area. A ride is not confirmed until a driver accepts it.

Payments and authorization

Where payment through the platform is enabled, a card payment is authorized (but not charged) at the time of booking. The payment is captured — meaning you are actually charged — once your ride is verified as complete. Payments are processed by Stripe. A platform fee may apply in addition to the fare; this will be shown before you confirm a payment-enabled booking.

Cancellations

You may cancel a ride request before a driver completes it. If a payment was authorized for a cancelled ride, the authorization is released rather than captured, subject to normal card network processing times. Repeated or late cancellations may affect your ability to use the platform.

Independent taxi companies

Each taxi company on the platform operates independently: it holds its own licensing, insures and maintains its own vehicles, and is responsible for its own drivers' conduct and compliance with applicable local regulations. Maritime Cabs reviews and approves taxi companies before they can receive bookings, but the ride itself is provided by that taxi company, not by Maritime Cabs.

Service availability

Ride availability depends on which taxi companies are actively covering your area at the time of your request. Maritime Cabs does not guarantee that a ride will be available at any given time or location.

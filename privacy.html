Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains what information Maritime Cabs collects and how it is used.

Who this covers

Maritime Cabs is a technology platform connecting passengers with independent local taxi companies in New Brunswick, Canada. This policy covers information collected through the Maritime Cabs app and this website. It does not cover the independent taxi companies' own internal record-keeping, which is their own responsibility.

Passenger information

To create an account and request rides, we collect information such as your name, phone number, and email address. This information is used to operate your account, connect you with a participating taxi company, and provide ride history and support.

Location information

To request a ride, show an estimated fare, and track a ride in progress, the app uses your pickup and destination location, and — while a ride is active — your live location is shared with your assigned driver and their taxi company so the ride can be completed. Location is not tracked when you do not have an active ride request.

Payment information

Card payments are processed by Stripe, a third-party payment processor. Maritime Cabs does not store your full card number. Payment is authorized when you request a ride and captured once the ride is verified as complete. See Stripe's own privacy policy for how Stripe handles payment data.

Ride and cancellation records

We keep a record of requested, completed, and cancelled rides, including fare and payment status, to operate the service, resolve disputes, and meet our own record-keeping obligations.

Independent taxi companies

Each taxi company on the platform is an independently operated, separately licensed business. A taxi company and its drivers can see the booking information needed to complete your ride (such as pickup and destination, and your live location during an active ride). Each taxi company is responsible for how it uses that information in the course of providing your ride.

Information sharing

We share information with the taxi company assigned to your ride, with our payment processor (Stripe) to process payment, and with service providers who help us operate the platform (such as our hosting and mapping providers). We do not sell passenger information.

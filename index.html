For Taxi Companies

Licensed independent taxi companies can join Maritime Cabs to receive bookings and manage drivers and dispatch operations. Maritime Cabs is a booking and dispatch technology platform — each participating taxi company operates under its own independent licensing and remains responsible for its own vehicles, drivers, and local regulatory compliance.

Payments
